* About these pictures
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: about
:END:

[[https://github.com/szymonwilczek/au-themes][au-themes]] are calm, low-glare colour themes for GNU
Emacs, made first of all for autistic people and for anyone who finds most
themes too loud to read for long. Code and the test suite live at [[https://github.com/szymonwilczek/au-themes][szymonwilczek/au-themes]]

The screenshots below show the themes as they are on the =main= branch today
(31.10.2026). They are taken in my everyday Emacs, not in a clean one: the font
is Typus Mono 92 (the story is in [[/writings/the-8-pixel-obsession-typus-mono][8-Pixel Obsession]]), the tabs come
from [[https://github.com/szymonwilczek/bufferline.el][bufferline.el]], and the mode line, Evil and the completion stack
are my own setup. So this is what the themes look like in real use, not
in a demo.

Each theme gets four pictures:

1. *Code*: Emacs Lisp from =au-themes.el= next to a small C file in
   =c-ts-mode=.
2. *Git*: Magit status with staged and unstaged changes, next to a
   commit that changed a palette.
3. *Dired and completion*: a Dired listing, a =*Help*= buffer, and =M-x=
   with Vertico and Marginalia.
4. *Org*: a release plan with headings, TODO keywords, tags, a table,
   a source block and a quote, next to the week agenda.

* The themes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: themes
:END:

*[[#light-themes][Light themes]]*

- [[#au-whispergrove-day][au-whispergrove-day]]: Muted greens and neutral tones for daylight.
- [[#au-whispergrove-morning][au-whispergrove-morning]]: A dimmer canvas than the other light themes, for early mornings or a room with little light.
- [[#au-aurum-day][au-aurum-day]]: Warm earth tones on a muted canvas.
- [[#au-parchment-day][au-parchment-day]]: Almost monochrome: italics and brightness steps instead of colour.
- [[#au-salina-day][au-salina-day]]: The coast in daylight: salt flats, sand dunes, deep cobalt ocean, driftwood and brine pink.
- [[#au-herbarium-day][au-herbarium-day]]: Pressed sage leaves, dried lavender, oak-bark ink and chamomile pollen.

*[[#dark-themes][Dark themes]]*

Best viewed on a dark page :)

- [[#au-whispergrove-night][au-whispergrove-night]]: Muted greens and earth tones for unlit rooms.
- [[#au-aurum-night][au-aurum-night]]: Amber and gold on a deep background, with the least blue light of the dark themes.
- [[#au-parchment-night][au-parchment-night]]: Almost monochrome: italics and brightness steps instead of colour.
- [[#au-salina-night][au-salina-night]]: Midnight ocean, dark basalt, phosphorescent turquoise and maritime coral.
- [[#au-herbarium-night][au-herbarium-night]]: Pressed sage, dried lavender, oak-gall ink, terracotta flora and chamomile pollen.
- [[#au-mysterium][au-mysterium]]: A Halloween night and an old painted van parked outside a haunted mansion: a near-black canvas, faded teal keywords, a dry yellow-green and burnt orange, with a muted purple over the region.
